|Term |Definition
|DGB |Short form of the name Dragons Guild Bern - the loose organization that organizes pnP / dnd events in Bern
|TTRPG |tabletop role-playing game - a game played usually offline at a table with a group of people where a GM plays the world and the players the heros in it.
|PNP |pen-and-paper role-playing game - alternative for TTRPG - see the TTRPG definition
|DnD / D&D |Dungeons and Dragons - a TTRPG by Wizards of the coast. Arguably the biggest and well known system in its genre.
|GM / DM |Game Master / Dungeon Master - is a person, the player who acts as a facilitator, arbitrator of rules and moderator for a group i.e. provides the story, the quest, displays the world the heros inhabit.
| Table | In the context of an event, of a game a "table" refers to a group of players that organize themselves around an adventure setup by a GM.
| Guild | The Discord term for an organization or "sever". See also_ https://docs.discord.com/developers/resources/guild[Discord Dev Docs - Guild]
